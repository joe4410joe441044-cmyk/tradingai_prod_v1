# Work CC to BB read-only handoff proposal

## Status and boundary

This is a CC-owned proposal for a future BB consumer. It is not an agreed BB
contract and no BB integration has been performed. The payload grants no runtime,
Money Management, strategy, order, or execution authority.

## Public CC interface

- Module: `backend.prop_trading.outcome_projection`
- Schema constant: `PROP_OUTCOME_READ_ONLY_CONTEXT_SCHEMA_VERSION`
- Function: `project_outcome_read_only_context(value)`
- Accepted input: a `CompleteEvaluationOutcome`, or its complete serialized mapping
- Output schema: `tradingai-prop-outcome-read-only-context/v1`
- Synthetic sample: `frontend/src/features/prop-accounts/generatedSyntheticOutcomes.json`
- Explicit generator: `PYTHONPATH=. python scripts/generate_prop_outcome_fixtures.py --output frontend/src/features/prop-accounts/generatedSyntheticOutcomes.json`

The function strict-deserializes the complete Outcome before projection. Nested
digests, Evidence Link v2, time cutoff, account graph, result/link consistency, and
derived candidate status must all validate. A raw dictionary is never converted to
a PASS display without that validation.

## Fields and interpretation

The v1 projection contains schema and source versions; synthetic and purpose flags;
account id/revision/execution mode/execution venue/program/plan/phase/currency scope; evaluated time and knowledge
cutoff; bundle, snapshot, baseline, observation, evidence-link, and outcome
references; provenance-validation scope; required-rule coverage; calculation and
candidate/freshness state; per-rule Decimal strings, units, reason codes, threshold, actual,
headroom/progress; and an explicit authority block.

`STRICTLY_VALIDATED` means structural, semantic, temporal, digest, and reference
consistency only. It does not authenticate the author, prove official rule evidence,
confirm a real account, certify Challenge completion, or authorize trading.
`executionAuthorityGranted`, `runtimeReady`, and `tradeReady` are always false.

Decimal values remain strings. Unknown values remain null. Currency and unit are
preserved. Machine-readable reason codes are not UI copy and should not be translated
in the payload. A future BB consumer must reject an unknown schema version, account
scope mismatch, authority promotion, malformed Decimal, or missing required field.

## Freshness and coverage

`evaluatedAt` is the calculation instant and `knowledgeCutoffAt` is the latest allowed
information time. Non-retroactive Complete Bundles require equality; retroactive
analysis remains explicitly distinguishable and non-operational. `coverage` retains
completeness, operational completeness, unresolved reasons, per-rule requirement
role/support, and evidence references. Missing or unsupported required coverage must
remain UNKNOWN/PARTIAL and cannot be inferred as PASS.

`freshnessState` is the state established when the Outcome was generated. It is not a
perpetual freshness guarantee. A consumer must compare the retained timestamps with
its own current UTC clock and authorized maximum-age policy at read time; expired,
missing, malformed, or unsupported freshness must fail closed and must not be treated
as a normal current projection. The fixed synthetic fixture date must not be advanced
to make fabricated data appear current.

Only typed codes and constrained opaque references are projected; observation source,
warnings/free-form messages, raw venue responses, UIDs, IPs, credentials, signatures,
and filesystem paths are not consumer context. This boundary prevents those fields
from being introduced by this projector, but it does not independently prove the
authenticity or confidentiality of upstream evidence.


## Synthetic scenarios and sample files

Six scenarios are generated by the wrapper and are not hand-authored alternatives to
the projection schema:

- `calculation_pass`: read-only calculation PASS without trading authority.
- `maximum_loss_block`: maximum-loss rule BLOCK.
- `required_information_unknown`: missing balance/equity produces required UNKNOWN.
- `unsupported_required_rule`: unsupported required coverage remains PARTIAL/UNKNOWN.
- `profit_target_reached`: target progress reaches PASS without Challenge certification.
- `pre_evaluation_validation_failure`: Bundle construction rejects and no Outcome exists.

Sample files are:
- `frontend/src/features/prop-accounts/generatedSyntheticOutcomes.json` — generated source of truth.
- `frontend/src/features/prop-accounts/propOutcomeFixtures.js` — fail-closed fixture consumer.
- `frontend/src/components/prop-accounts/PropOutcomeCard.jsx` — authority-free fixture display.

## BB owner responsibilities

BB owns its future consumer location, current-clock freshness policy, account/request
scope binding, AI-context allowlist, error presentation, and integration tests. It must
keep missing/stale/partial/unsupported states visible, grant no authority, and must not
route raw free text or secrets into AI context. CC does not prescribe or duplicate a
second BB schema.

The providing preservation commit is recorded in the CC-3I report after commit
creation. This document is handoff-ready only; BB acceptance is not performed.

## Proposed BB acceptance tests

1. Accept the checked-in v1 synthetic sample without network, registry, storage, or runtime access.
2. Reject any schema other than `tradingai-prop-outcome-read-only-context/v1`.
3. Reject an account id/revision/execution-mode/execution-venue/plan/phase mismatch with the BB request scope; never fall back to another account.
4. Reject `executionAuthorityGranted`, `runtimeReady`, or `tradeReady` when not false.
5. Preserve Decimal strings and distinguish null, zero, negative, and invalid values.
6. Preserve PASS, BLOCK, UNKNOWN, NOT_APPLICABLE, incomplete coverage, and reason codes.
7. Treat target progress/reached as progress only, never Challenge completion.
8. Treat calculation PASS/candidate state as context only, never an order or strategy gate.
9. Reject result/link, cutoff, candidate-status, or account tampering before projection.
10. Verify regeneration produces byte-identical JSON and performs no import-time write.
11. Retain evaluated time, cutoff, and generation-time freshness, then independently apply current-time staleness at consumption.
12. Reject missing projection/required fields and keep unsupported or required UNKNOWN coverage visible.
13. Exclude raw free text, credentials, signatures, UIDs, IPs, venue responses, and paths from AI context.

BB ownership, its schema, and its files remain unchanged. Future integration requires
separate BB-owner review and authorization.
